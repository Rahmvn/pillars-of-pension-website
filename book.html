Pillars of the Pension System in Nigeria

By Saheed Adekunle Imran

This publication examines the evolution of pension administration, from international frameworks to the policies and reforms shaping Nigeria’s retirement system.

It explores the relationship between public and private pension arrangements, mandatory and voluntary schemes, and the informal support systems that contribute to retirement security. Particular attention is given to regulation, investment, governance, and the challenges of building a sustainable pension system.

Drawing together policy analysis and practical considerations, the book provides a resource for pension professionals, policymakers, legal and financial practitioners, researchers, and students.
