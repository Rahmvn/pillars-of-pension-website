Saheed Adekunle Imran

Ph.D  ·  FCA

His work brings together academic research and professional practice in accounting, finance and pension administration.

He studied Accounting at Obafemi Awolowo University, earned an MSc in Finance from London Metropolitan University, and completed a PhD in Accounting and Finance at Ahmadu Bello University.

His research and speaking engagements have addressed financial management reform, pension funds management and related topics. He has also contributed academic publications and peer-review work.
